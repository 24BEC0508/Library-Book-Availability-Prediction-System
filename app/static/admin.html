Methodology & Statistical Transparency

Model Shrinkage Reality: The 12.5M row loan transaction dataset has 98.71% target zeros, causing linear regression OLS coefficients to shrink towards the historical unconditional mean (≈ 0.024 checkouts/week).

Operational Hybrid Strategy: To ensure predictions respond dynamically to live student movement without requiring continuous model retraining, the system evaluates: (1) Recent observed borrowing run-rates (weighted 24h, 3d, 7d velocity), (2) Department historical exam surge rates for CAT-1, CAT-2, and FAT, (3) The academic linear regression prior.
