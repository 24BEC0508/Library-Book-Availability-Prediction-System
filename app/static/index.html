Institutional Borrowing Guidelines & Policy

All physical catalog holdings are issued on a strictly First-Come, First-Served (FCFS) basis upon presenting a valid student identification badge at the central circulation desk.

How Demand Forecasts Assist You: The library's machine learning model evaluates recent borrowing velocities and university exam calendars to project anticipated 7-day demand and estimate when popular titles might stock out. This allows you to visit the library early before peak exam rushes. Forecasts represent statistical estimates, not held reservations.
